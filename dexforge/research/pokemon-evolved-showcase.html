Pokémon Evolved — Kanto Starter Lines

Interactive showcase of Bulbasaur, Charmander & Squirtle evolution chains with 3D-rotating sprites and elemental power animations.

About Pokémon Evolved (ROM Hack)

  • Creator: AtSign8877 & team — GitHub · Official site
  • Base ROM: Pokémon LeafGreen v1.1 (USA)
  • Released: March 26, 2025 · Status: Complete
  • Concept: Every original Gen I Pokémon gains at least two more evolution stages — expanding the Pokédex from 151 to 354 Pokémon.
  • Sprites: All new evolutions have custom front/back sprites, cries, and shiny variants (art by calicorn, katten, jordanos, xiapher & others).
  • Gameplay: EXP All on, physical/special split, infinite Rare Candies in PC, trade/friendship evos replaced by level-ups, all stones sold in Celadon.
Stages 1–3 use official FireRed/LeafGreen sprites (PokeAPI). Stages 4–5 are canvas-generated approximations inspired by Pokémon Evolved's extended fan-art evolutions — open the ROM or visit pkmnevolved.com for exact in-game sprites.
